Capstone Big Idea:

Integrating AI-driven edge computing with the Internet of Things (IoT) for next-
generation industrial automation, educational robotics, or assistive medical
technologies is the main objective of this year's challenge. Students must design a
system that analyzes the current situation—including the necessary sensing,
measurements, and communication with information/data sources—to formulate
AI-driven solutions and intelligent techniques aimed at expanding the industrial
base, delivering medical services, or enhancing the learning environment. The
proposed solution/prototype must include sensing, wireless data transfer to the
cloud, advanced communication tools, and AI platforms.

Essential Question: How can integrating AI tools with information technology help
develop reliable and intelligent devices that improve the industrial base, provide
medical services, or enhance the learning environment?

\fDesign Challenge:

Recently, AI has significantly improved device performance across a wide range of
applications. An AI-based device can analyze and interpret variable inputs and
adjust its performance accordingly. Therefore, students must design a device/system
that analyzes the current situation in one of the challenges under consideration.
The proposed device must perform the necessary sensing, measurements, and
communication with information/data sources to support AI-driven solutions. These
solutions should help expand the industrial base, deliver reliable medical services,
or enhance the learning environment. The schematic diagram of the capstone idea

\fPathways
The following are examples of the challenges under consideration. However, the
students are not restricted to these examples.


   A. Industrial Base
   • Fully automated production lines.
   • Product quality inspection and control.
   • Any relevant application to increase the industrial base, with the approval of
       the school board.


   B. Educational Environment
   • A device that incorporates voice and text to facilitate foreign-language
       learning.
   • A device for automatically grading homework and providing feedback to
       improve students' performance and support activities related to the
       educational process.
   • A device that assists with programming in general and AI-based projects in
       particular.
   • Any relevant device/application that facilitates the educational environment,
       with the approval of the school board.


   C. Medical Services
   • A device for people with disabilities that continuously monitors the user's
       condition and, in critical situations, notifies the next of kin, responsible
       medical personnel, and a nearby hospital. The device could also contact
       emergency services when immediate intervention is required.
   • AI-based smart glasses that assist people who are blind with daily activities.

Design Requirements:

The design must meet the following requirements:

   • Students must identify at least four measurable design requirements,
      including response time and system accuracy, for their prototype. Students
      must also select two additional measurable design requirements based on the
      application.
   • The prototype must use Information and Communication Technology (ICT),
      IoT, and AI platforms.
   • The prototype must contain hardware components.
   • Students must provide datasets and a rubric for machine/deep learning
      algorithms during the exhibition.
   • Students must provide evidence of the accuracy of the AI model, which must
      be at least 80%.
   • Students must conduct and document sensor calibration.
   • Functional prototypes must be testable, operational, reliable, and portable on
      exhibition day. The team should also bring a hard copy of each team
      member's logbook.

Constraints:

   • The whole system (software and hardware) must be testable.
